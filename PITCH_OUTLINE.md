## Slide 2: The Enterprise Pain
* **Title:** The $100B Reactive Lag
* **Bullet Points:**
  * Modern supply chains span 5,000+ nodes across disparate ERPs, port APIs, and carrier portals.
  * Passive dashboards alert operators **after** delays happen.
  * Late reactions force emergency air freight, assembly line shutdowns, and customer churn.
* **Speaker Notes:** Highlight that the root issue isn't lack of data—it's that humans are overwhelmed by disconnected alerts and cannot mentally simulate multi-step cascade effects.

## Slide 3: The Paradigm Shift: Counterfactual Resilience
* **Title:** Don't Just Alert. Simulate & Act.
* **Bullet Points:**
  * Sense: Ingest federated telemetry instantly.
  * Simulate: Roll out future "what-if" branches before committing money.
  * Decide: Multi-criteria optimization balancing cost vs. risk.
  * Gate: Human-in-the-loop governance on consequential capital deployments.
* **Speaker Notes:** Frame Indus Flow as an active copilot that tests decisions in a sandbox before executing them in reality.

## Slide 5: The Cognitive Engine — Meterless World Model
* **Title:** Why World Models Matter for Enterprise Agents
* **Bullet Points:**
  * LLMs alone hallucinate physical dynamics and lack grounded state estimation.
  * Meterless World Model tracks discrete inventory buffers, carrier delays, and port bottlenecks across $N$-day horizons.
  * Calculates calibrated risk probabilities ($0.0$ to $1.0$) and confidence intervals.
* **Speaker Notes:** Show a snapshot of the trajectory simulation comparison from the dashboard.
